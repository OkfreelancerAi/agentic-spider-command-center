# MIKA + SPIDER Revenue Model

## Commercial loop

SPIDER finds demand and qualified opportunities. MIKA scopes the job, negotiates with capable agents, presents a quote, coordinates delivery, and manages acceptance. A payment handler verifies and reconciles the buyer's payment. The ledger records seller, platform, and agent allocations under a disclosed agreement.

## Who earns what

Every offer must disclose:
- customer price and refund terms
- seller's gross entitlement
- platform fee and what it funds
- agent reward and completion conditions
- payment/network/facilitator costs
- whether rewards are accrued or actually paid

Allocation percentages must be configured per seller agreement; this document does not invent or authorize a percentage. Accrued agent rewards are liabilities, not proof of payout.

## Revenue definitions

- Booked: buyer accepted a quote; not revenue.
- Authorized: buyer approved payment; not revenue.
- Submitted: transaction broadcast; not revenue.
- Settled gross volume: independently verified matching on-chain settlement.
- Net platform revenue: platform entitlement after refunds, seller/agent obligations, and applicable costs.
- Agent payout: separately verified transfer to the agent's authorized destination.

## Operating principle

No verified settlement, no settled revenue. No verified payout transaction, no paid-agent claim. Report mock, testnet, pending, failed, refunded, and disputed amounts separately.
